# Privacy Policy

**Last updated:** March 12, 2026
**Version:** 1.0.0

---

## What We Collect

We collect:
- **Account info** — your name, email, and relationship details you share during onboarding.
- **Session content** — your private vents, the insights we extract, and commitments you make together.
- **App usage data** — which screens you visit, how often you start sessions. Never your actual content.
- **Device info** — your operating system, app version, and crash logs so we can fix bugs.

We do **not** collect:
- Location data
- Your contacts
- Any data from outside the Relate app

---

## Your Private Vents

Your private vent is never shown to your partner in its raw form. Ever.

Your partner only ever sees the synthesized Unpacking — the shared insights the AI generates from both perspectives.

We do not use your vent content to train AI models. Your words are yours.

---

## Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal data to anyone.

We share data only with:
- **AI processing provider (Anthropic)** to generate mediation responses — your session content is sent via encrypted API calls and is not used to train AI models.
- **Infrastructure providers** who run our servers, under strict data processing agreements.
- **Payment processors**, for subscription billing only. They never see your session content.
- **Law enforcement**, only if compelled by a valid court order or subpoena.

If we ever receive a legal request for your data, we will notify you as permitted by law before complying.

---

## The Subpoena Clause

**Important:** Relate is not a licensed therapy service. Your session content does not carry therapist-client privilege.

This means session data could theoretically be requested in legal proceedings such as divorce or custody cases. We will always resist such requests to the extent the law allows, but we cannot guarantee confidentiality in a legal context.

If you have concerns about this, we recommend speaking with a lawyer before using Relate during an active legal dispute.

---

## Data Retention

- **Session content:** retained for 12 months from your last activity, then deleted automatically.
- **Long-term relationship learnings:** retained until you delete them or close your account.
- **Account data:** deleted within 30 days of account closure.

You can delete all your data at any time from **Settings > Privacy > Delete My Data**. Both partners must request deletion for shared session data to be removed.

---

## Children

Relate is not for anyone under 18. We do not knowingly collect data from minors. If we discover a user is under 18, we will immediately delete their account and all associated data.

---

## Your Rights (GDPR)

If you are in the European Economic Area, you have the right to:
- Access your personal data
- Correct inaccurate data
- Request deletion of your data
- Object to processing of your data
- Data portability

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at privacy@relate.app
