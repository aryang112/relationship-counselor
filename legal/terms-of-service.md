## What Relate Is (and Isn't)

Relate is a relationship support tool powered by AI. It is designed to help couples understand each other better during conflict.

Relate is **not**:
- A licensed therapy or counseling service
- A crisis intervention service
- A substitute for professional mental health support
- A legal mediation service

Using Relate does not create a therapist-client relationship, a counselor-client relationship, or any other professional relationship of any kind.

---

## Who Can Use Relate

You must be:
- **18 years of age or older**
- Using Relate **voluntarily** (not under pressure from a partner)
- Not currently involved in an active legal dispute where session content could be used as evidence (see our Privacy Policy, "The Subpoena Clause")

Relate is designed for two consenting adults in a romantic relationship. It is not designed for use in situations involving abuse, coercion, or safety concerns.

---

## Safety

Relate is **not appropriate** if:
- You feel unsafe with your partner
- Your partner is controlling your use of the app
- There is any history of physical, emotional, or sexual abuse in this relationship

If any of the above apply, please contact a professional service. Attempting to mediate in these situations can increase risk.

We reserve the right to terminate accounts that we believe are being used in unsafe relationship dynamics.

---

## About Our AI

Relate uses artificial intelligence to analyze your input and generate insights. This AI:
- Can make mistakes
- Does not have clinical training
- Does not know your full relationship history unless you provide it
- May occasionally misread emotional context

Do not make major relationship decisions based solely on what Relate tells you. Use it as one input among many.

---

## Both Partners Must Consent

Both partners must individually create accounts and agree to these Terms before participating in a shared session.

You may not submit a vent or response on behalf of your partner. You may not create a fake partner account to simulate a session. Violations will result in immediate account termination.
